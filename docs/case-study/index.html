The problem

A life and protection insurer has more candidate AI use cases than it can deliver. The highest-value ideas are often the ones that act on customers, through pricing, acceptance or claims, where an error harms people. Governance is often added after a build instead of deciding whether it should be built.

The approach

1Assess

14 AI use cases across distribution, underwriting, policy admin, servicing and claims.

2Gate

Each must pass the insurer's internal AI risk tier – its own appetite measure, not a regulatory classification.

3Prioritise and govern

Eligible use cases are ranked by weighted score; two shortlisted ones carry worked controls and agent accountability.

What the gate changed

At Medium appetite, 11 of 14 use cases are eligible. The gate removes 3: VC-02, VC-14 high; VC-03 unacceptable. At High appetite the top-ranked use case would be VC-02 (90.0); at Medium it is not eligible, so no weighting can bring it back.

The two worked agents

VC-01

Underwriting Evidence Summariser

Medium

What it does: Summarises application disclosures and medical evidence into a structured summary with page citations for an underwriter, who makes every decision; it never sets terms, exclusions or premium.

The agent may

  • Recommend: read-only: application disclosures, medical evidence pages, underwriting rules library (accountable role: Underwriter)
  • Stop: read-only: application disclosures, medical evidence pages, underwriting rules library; agent may halt the summary and escalate when evidence is missing, illegible or conflicting (accountable role: Underwriter)

The agent may not

  • Approve: no agent permission; decision approval held by the underwriter in the underwriting decision record (accountable role: Underwriter)
  • Execute: no agent write; permission to apply terms in the underwriting system held by the senior underwriter (accountable role: Senior underwriter)
  • Override: no agent permission; override authority held by the chief underwriter under the documented underwriting exception process (accountable role: Chief underwriter)
Pass: 9Fail: 1Not tested: 2Exceptions: 1

Open findings

  • CT-VC-01-02 — The accuracy of each summary must be measured against a sample verified by an underwriter. Remediation: Correct the extraction errors found, add them to the regression set and re-run the monthly 150-summary accuracy sample by January 2027.
  • EX-01 — Evidence held outside the repository; exception approved at the underwriting governance forum pending export.

VC-05

Policy-Admin Data Exception Agent

Medium

What it does: Flags conflicting policy records, including benefit amount mismatches, lapsed policies still collecting premium, commission split errors and conflicting life-assured details after a platform migration, and recommends an evidence-backed correction for a policy data owner to approve without writing to the policy administration system.

The agent may

  • Recommend: read-only: policy schedule, policy admin record, premium collection status, commission split (accountable role: Policy data owner)
  • Stop: read-only: policy schedule, policy admin record, premium collection status, commission split; agent may halt the correction and escalate when evidence is missing, illegible or conflicting (accountable role: Policy data owner)

The agent may not

  • Approve: no agent write; correction approval held by the policy data owner in the policy correction approval queue (accountable role: Policy data owner)
  • Execute: no agent write; policy administration system write permission held by the policy administration manager (accountable role: Policy administration manager)
  • Override: no agent permission; override authority held by the head of policy administration under the documented policy administration exception process (accountable role: Head of policy administration)
Pass: 8Fail: 2Not tested: 2Exceptions: 1

Open findings

  • CT-VC-05-02 — Every recommendation must identify the conflicting records and the rule it applied. Remediation: Backfill citations for all open recommendations and enable the pre-approval citation block by January 2027.
  • CT-VC-05-03 — Matching thresholds for migrated records must be calibrated on a labelled sample so genuine matches are found without merging unrelated policies. Remediation: Recalibrate the migrated-record matching thresholds on the current labelled sample and record model owner approval by February 2027.
  • EX-02 — Evidence held outside the repository; exception approved at the policy administration governance forum pending export.
